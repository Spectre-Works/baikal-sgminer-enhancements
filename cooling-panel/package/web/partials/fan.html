Automatic curve and recovery behavior

Below 40°C: 10% · 40–44°C: 25% · 45–47°C: 40% · 48–50°C: 65% · 51°C and above: 100%.

Increases are immediate at the next governor check. Reductions require 60 seconds at least 2°C below the current step threshold and move one step at a time. Invalid or missing board telemetry forces full cooling in automatic mode. The original thermal cutoff remains unchanged.
